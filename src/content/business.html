Business FAQ

Can you source products directly from Chinese factories?

Depending on the product, quantity and requirements, we can help search for potential Chinese manufacturers and factories.

Do I need a product link?

No. You can send us a product photo, description or sourcing requirement.

Is buying directly from a factory always cheaper?

Not necessarily. The best source depends on the product, quantity, specifications, customization requirements and shipping. We help explore suitable sourcing options.

Can you help with wholesale?

Yes. We can help with supplier sourcing, purchasing, basic QC and international shipping coordination for eligible orders.

Can you help with private label or custom products?

We can help search for potential Chinese manufacturers and coordinate sourcing inquiries. Custom manufacturing and sampling may require a separate quotation.

Do you guarantee product quality?

No. Our standard QC is a basic inspection for visible condition, quantity and obvious discrepancies. It is not a laboratory, technical or certification inspection.

Do you guarantee the supplier?

No. We can help research and compare sourcing options, but supplier performance and product quality depend on the actual supplier and product.

⚖️ Eligible Products: We can help source a wide range of eligible products, subject to product availability, payment, shipping requirements and applicable laws and regulations. Dangerous, illegal, counterfeit and restricted goods follow our standard policy.

🚚 International Shipping Coordination: We help coordinate international shipping after your products are received and prepared in China. Shipping options, costs and transit times depend on destination, package weight, dimensions, shipping method, carrier and customs requirements.

🛃 Customs & Taxes: Duties, taxes and customs charges depend on the destination country, product type, declared value and applicable regulations. Unless specifically included in your quote, duties and taxes are not included in the estimated total.

💳 Flexible Payment Options: Available payment methods may depend on order amount, destination, currency and transaction eligibility. PayPal may be available for eligible transactions; PayPal terms, eligibility and policies apply.
