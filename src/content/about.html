About Us

Changsha Baichen Enterprise Development Co., Ltd. was founded in 2020 and is based in Hunan, China — one of the world’s major manufacturing and supply-chain hubs.

We operate across two complementary business areas.

Our first business is China sourcing and supply chain management. Through BuyTCN, we help individuals and businesses worldwide purchase products from Chinese marketplaces, suppliers, wholesalers, and manufacturers. From product sourcing and supplier communication to purchasing, quality checks, and international shipping, we handle the China-side work for our customers.

Our second business is LED manufacturing. We are an investor in Changsha Bshine Optoelectronics Technology Co., Ltd., a specialized LED manufacturer producing LED light strips for commercial light boxes and signage, stretch ceilings, outdoor food trucks, and other commercial lighting applications.

With experience in both sourcing and manufacturing, we understand the China supply chain from the inside. Our team combines practical industry experience with reliable supplier and production resources to help customers solve purchasing, sourcing, and manufacturing challenges in China.

Whether you need to find a product, source from a supplier, or develop a manufacturing solution, we are here to help.
